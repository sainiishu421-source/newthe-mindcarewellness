Your Sanctuary is Secure

At Mind Care, we believe your mental wellness journey is deeply personal. Our commitment to your privacy is woven into every layer of our platform. We ensure your data remains protected, private, and entirely in your control.

Our Core Promise

We will never sell your personal or mental health data to third parties. Your assessments, journal entries, and progress are encrypted and accessible only by you and your authorized healthcare providers.

All sensitive information exchanged between your device and our servers is protected using state-of-the-art 256-bit AES encryption. This is the same standard used by leading financial institutions and healthcare providers globally, ensuring your data is scrambled and unreadable to anyone trying to intercept it.

Your data is securely stored on enterprise-grade servers with redundant backups. We utilize secure, compliant cloud infrastructure located in heavily monitored data centers. Access to these servers is strictly controlled and limited to essential engineering personnel under rigorous security protocols.

We are fully compliant with global privacy regulations including GDPR and HIPAA. This means you have complete control over your data.

  • The right to access all data we hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • The right to export your data in a portable format.
  • The right to withdraw consent for specific data processing at any time.

We maintain a strict zero-sharing policy for personal mental health data with advertisers or data brokers. We only share essential, anonymized technical data with trusted service providers (like secure hosting services) necessary to operate the app, all of whom are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
